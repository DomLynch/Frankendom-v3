## 1. HUD: V3 against V2

**Where the HUD lives.**
- **V3:** V1's HUD as lifted.
  - `src/fight/hud.ts` (298 lines) paints the bars, labels, button states and damage numbers.
  - `src/fight/input.ts` turns on V1's one-button strike: row `input.one-button` = 1 in `input-rows.json`. That row brings in `src/fight/touch-layout.ts`, V1's "layout A", which places and labels the buttons from `touch-layout-defs.json`.
  - `src/fight/mana-bar.ts` adds a MANA bar, because row `manaMax` = 100.
  - `src/client/main.ts` writes the opponent's name and the build label.
  - `src/style.css` is V1's stylesheet.
  - `data/fight.json readyRing` adds the ring from #32.
- **V2:** `index.html` (the `#hud` markup and inline CSS), `src/client/input.js` (it builds the pads from `data/controls.json`) and `src/client/main.js` (bar widths, the cooldown dim, the result card).

**The big finding: the button layout is already V2's.** V2's `controls.json` says it copies V1's live "layout A". V3 runs that same layout from V1's own rows, with the same numbers:
- STRIKE is 72 px, centred 104 px from the right edge and 128 px from the bottom.
- The zone ring is 146 px across.
- The other buttons are 50 px (KICK is 44 px), with a 3 px gap, at the same angles.

So the gaps are in the **look** of the buttons, the **bars**, the **stick** and the **end of the fight**, not in where the buttons sit.

## 2. First-visit weight (live, 553faa6)

**How it was measured.**
- **Browser waterfall:** Chromium on the VPS, run with `/root/f3v-load/measure2.mjs`, the same harness that #13 used. Viewport 375x812, iPhone user agent, cache off. Run twice:
  - unthrottled ("fast");
  - "4G" = 9 Mbps down, 3 Mbps up, 150 ms latency.
  - Both runs: 46 network requests (plus 22 in-page `blob:` reads of zero bytes), **6.88 MB over the wire**.
- **Per-file check:** every URL in that waterfall, plus the lazy chunks the bundle can ask for, was fetched with `curl -s -H 'Accept-Encoding: …' -o /dev/null -w '%{size_download}'` three ways (identity, gzip, br) and with `curl -sI` for the encoding and cache headers.
- **Results** are in the table below. The script and raw output are in the session scratchpad; the commands are above, so anyone can repeat them.

**Totals**

**What is inside `main.js`.** The bundle was rebuilt on the VPS at 553faa6 with an esbuild metafile: 1,215,456 bytes minified, 417 KB gzip, from 400 source files.
- **`src/fight/blade-paths.ts` is 379 KB of it (31%).** It holds baked blade sweeps for 11 hero weapons and 7 beast rigs. V3 fights with two of them. Cutting the table down to the longsword and gladius sweeps takes it from 128 KB gzip to 18 KB (measured on the JSON), **saving about 110 KB on the wire.**
- The other big pieces: bundled data rows 168 KB; the 9 signature effects the Centurion never ships, 38 KB; spell, missile and mana modules, 47 KB; `catalogue-data.ts`, 48 KB (all minified).

**Proposed target: 5 MB first visit, about 4.5 MB before playable, then 4.5 MB as a stretch.** The reasoning:
- **The bodies are not where the weight is any more.** Dom's ruling is 6-7 MB per body. Today's two packed bodies are 2.8 MB raw each and 2.82 MB together on the wire. Read the ruling as a ceiling with plenty of room, not as a budget to spend.
- **The section 10 rig swap would add weight, not cut it, as the files sit.** The 106-rig files on opentrident.org measured **5.34 MB raw / 3.36 MB gzip for Psyche (ch22)** and **3.77 / 2.88 MB for the Minotaur (ch25)**. That is 6.24 MB on the wire against today's 2.82 MB, **+3.4 MB**, and those files carry only 12 clips each, not V1's 27 and up. Section 10's saving came from replacing veteran (7.32 MB) and carriers (3.04 MB); #31 has already taken that saving. **Rule for the swap:** the 106 bodies must go through V3's own `scripts/optimize-glb.mjs` (meshopt, int8 normals, jpegtran) before they ship, and must keep the two bodies within about 3.5 MB on the wire together (**est.** from today's packing ratio).
- **The sword textures are the one big, cheap cut.** On their own they bring 6.88 MB down to about 5.2-5.5 MB.

## 3. scene.ts split plan (plan only)

**Size today.** `src/fight/scene.ts` is **1,243 lines** at 553faa6; the plan aimed for about 600. It has **73 import lines**:
- 63 named modules;
- 10 side-effect imports that register signature effects.

Almost all of it is one function, `createScene` (lines 95-1241). It returns an object with about 45 members. **The V3 driver (`src/client/main.ts`) uses 14 of them:** `render`, `renderer`, `yaw`, `project`, `startStandoff`, `restartStandoff`, `setTier`, `setPlayerTier`, `finishPhase`, `stopTour`, `previousFinisher`, `hamstrungInstalled`, `executionInstalled`, and the status callback.
